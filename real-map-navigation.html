Auto Rickshaw Rush

Drive a three-wheeler in a 3D driving world with real Mumbai route guidance. Pick up fares, beat the clock, dodge traffic. Keep left!
GoalStop at the yellow beacon to pick up a passenger, then the green beacon to drop them. Faster and smoother = bigger fare. PhoneHold your phone like a steering wheel in landscape and tilt it. ◀ ▶ buttons also work. GAS / BRAKE on the right. Brake again at a standstill to reverse. DesktopArrow keys or WASD. Space brakes, H horn, L lights, C camera, P pause. WatchFuel (collect red cans), body damage from crashes, and 3 late fares = game over.
